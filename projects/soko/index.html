Soko

made October 2026

Push every box onto a goal.

How to play

  • Arrow keys or WASD. Hold to keep moving
  • On a phone: swipe, or the buttons under the board
  • All players move together; a blocked player stays put
  • The edge is solid
  • undo (Z), restart (R)

Making levels

  • make: paint walls, floor, goals, boxes and players. Right-click paints floor
  • check: whether it can be solved
  • test, keep, share
  • Standard Sokoban text format
