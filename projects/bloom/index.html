Bloom

made September 2026

One click opens one bloom. Everything the bloom touches opens too, and so on, for as long as it keeps finding something. Each go pays pollen, pollen buys upgrades, and the upgrades are the game. It starts pitiful on purpose.

How to play

  • Click once, anywhere — where and when is the whole decision
  • Whatever the chain opens pays pollen; spend it in the shop, then go again
  • Nothing is ever lost — a bad go just pays less
  • Past the sixth link the multiplier starts climbing, and the climb itself gets steeper
  • Grey stones never open. Half the field is stone to begin with; clear them out
  • Gold pays five times a plain dot, blue opens slowly but huge, purple throws off two more blooms
  • A long chain slows time down so you can watch it happen
