Bloom

made September 2026

One click opens one bloom. Everything the bloom touches opens too, and so on, for as long as it keeps finding something. Clear the round, take an upgrade, go again — until a round beats you and the run is over.

How to play

  • Click once, anywhere — where and when is the whole decision
  • Open enough dots to meet the round's quota
  • Clear it and you pick one of three upgrades, which last as long as the run does
  • Miss it and the run ends; everything you picked up goes with it
  • Gold pays five times a plain dot, blue opens slowly but huge, purple throws off two more blooms
  • Grey stones never open. There are more of them every round, so the dots that matter get further apart
  • A long chain slows time down so you can watch it happen
  • Past the fifth link the multiplier starts climbing, and every link after that is worth more than the last
