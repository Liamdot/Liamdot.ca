Soko

made October 2026

Push every box onto a goal. You can push but never pull, and only one box at a time — so a box in a corner stays there.

How to play

  • Arrow keys or WASD to move; on a phone, swipe the board or use the buttons under it. Hold a direction to keep going
  • Some levels have more than one of you. Every press moves all of you the same way at once, and anyone who's blocked just stays put while the rest carry on
  • The edge of a level is as solid as a wall
  • undo takes back a move (Z) and restart starts again (R) — the board greys for a moment so you can see it went backwards
  • levels lists everything there is to play, built in or made by you

Making levels

  • Press make and paint with walls, floor, goals, boxes and players — right-click paints floor
  • No need to wall the outside in: the edge is solid. Put down as many players as you like
  • check tells you what's wrong with it, and tries every way of playing it to see whether it can be solved at all
  • test plays it, keep saves it to your levels
  • share gives you a link with the whole level in it — whoever opens it plays your level, and can keep it
  • Levels are plain text in the usual Sokoban format, so you can paste in ones from elsewhere too
