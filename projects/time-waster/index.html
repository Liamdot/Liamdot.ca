Time Waster

made September 2026

It counts how long you've had this open, and tells you what you could have done instead. That's the whole game.

How it works

  • It counts for as long as the page is open, tab in front or not
  • Your total is kept between visits
  • Put a name in to appear on the leaderboard
  • The server only believes time that could really have passed, so there's no point cheating
