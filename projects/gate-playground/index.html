Gate Playground

made September 2026

Build circuits out of logic gates and see what they do.

How to use

  • Drag a part from the toolbar onto the board
  • Drag from one pin to another to wire them up
  • Click an IN switch to flip it between 0 and 1
  • Drag a wire off an input pin to disconnect it
  • Right-click something (or select it and press Delete) to remove it
