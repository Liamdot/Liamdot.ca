Sokoball

made October 2026

Fill every goal, then get a player to a finish. Balls can be pushed but not pulled. Push a ball into another ball and the far one rolls away until something stops it.

How to play

  • The levels are doors on a map. Walk onto one and press Enter, Space or E (or enter) to play it
  • Solving a level frees the grey balls that block the way to the next door. Push them out of the way to get on
  • Solving a level takes you back to the map, and map (Esc) leaves a level without solving it
  • Move with the arrow keys or WASD, and hold a direction to keep moving
  • Goals and holes are holes in the floor. A ball pushed or rolled into one drops in and fills it, and then it's floor you can walk on
  • A player who walks into an open hole is stuck in it. Walking into a stuck player, or pushing a ball that's touching them, knocks them out. If every player is stuck, undo or restart
  • Grates can only be crossed rolling. Walk onto one, push a ball onto one, or end a roll on one, and it falls through. If every player is stuck or gone, undo or restart
  • Goals, with the red rim, are the ones that have to be filled. Plain holes are just in the way, and filling one costs a ball
  • Pushing a ball into another works like a Newton's cradle: the ball you push stays put, and the last ball in the row rolls until it hits something or drops into a hole. If the last in the row is a player stuck in a hole, they get knocked out instead
  • On a phone, swipe the board or use the buttons below it
  • When a level has several players, they all move together. A player that's blocked stays where it is
  • The finishes are lowered grey flags until every goal is filled, then they go up and turn green. The level is won as soon as one player is standing on a green flag
  • A player stuck in a goal counts as filling it, for as long as they stay there
  • Some levels have more balls than goals, so not every ball has to be used
  • A ball can go anywhere a player can, finishes and doors included. A ball on a finish or a door has to be moved off before it can be used
  • The edge of a level is solid, like a wall
  • undo (Z) takes back a move, and restart (R) starts the level again. On the map, restart puts every ball back where it started, in case one ends up in the way
  • my levels lists the levels you've made or saved

Making levels

  • make opens the editor. Paint walls, floor, goals, balls, players and finishes; right-click paints floor
  • A level needs a finish a player can reach. Balls and goals are optional: with no goals, the finishes are open from the start
  • check works out whether the puzzle can be solved
  • test plays it, and save to my levels keeps it in this browser
  • share makes a link that anyone can use to play it
  • Levels use the standard Sokoban text format, so levels from elsewhere can be pasted in
  • Clearing your save resets your progress and the map, but keeps your saved levels
