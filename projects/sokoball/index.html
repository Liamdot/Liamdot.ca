Sokoball

made October 2026

Fill every goal, then get a player to a finish. Balls can be pushed but not pulled. Push a ball into another ball and the far one rolls away until something stops it.

Controls

  • Move with the arrow keys or WASD, and hold a direction to keep moving. On a phone, swipe the board or use the buttons below it
  • On the map, stand on a door and press Enter, Space or E (or enter) to play its level
  • map (Esc) leaves a level without solving it
  • undo (Z) takes back a move, and restart (R) starts again

Making levels

  • make opens the editor. Paint walls, floor, goals, balls, players and finishes; right-click paints floor
  • A level needs a finish a player can reach. Balls and goals are optional: with no goals, the finishes are open from the start
  • check works out whether the puzzle can be solved, and show solution then plays the shortest way
  • The + and − on each side of the level grow and shrink it
  • test plays it, and save to my levels keeps it in this browser
  • share makes a link that anyone can use to play it
  • Levels use the standard Sokoban text format, so levels from elsewhere can be pasted in
  • Clearing your save resets your progress and the map, but keeps your saved levels
