Bloom

made September 2026

One click opens one bloom. Everything the bloom touches opens too, and so on, for as long as it keeps finding something.

How to play

  • Click once, anywhere — timing and place are the whole decision
  • Open enough dots to clear the level
  • Every dot pays petals; petals buy permanent upgrades
  • Gold pays five, blue opens slowly but wider, purple throws off two more blooms
  • A long chain slows time down so you can watch it happen
