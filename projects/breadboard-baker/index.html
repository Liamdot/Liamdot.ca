Breadboard Baker

made September 2026

Build a circuit on a breadboard that knows how a breadboard works, and have it tell you what you've got wrong before the smoke does.

How to use

  • Pick a part, click one hole, then click another — that's it placed
  • Chips go across the middle gap; click where you want pin 1
  • Click a button to press it; right-click anything to remove it
  • Select a resistor and press r to change its value
  • The rails are live: + is 5V, − is ground

What it checks

  • LEDs with nothing to limit the current, and LEDs in backwards
  • Chips with no power or no ground
  • Inputs left floating — they read high, which hides the mistake
  • Two outputs driving the same clip, or an output wired to a rail
  • The rails shorted together
