Plan a year of university courses without the spreadsheet.

How to use

  • Courses: add each course and every section it's offered in (lectures, labs, tutorials)
  • Timetables: see every combination that doesn't clash
  • Requirements: check you're taking what you need

Everything is saved in this browser. Use export to keep a backup.
