Gate Playground

made September 2026

Build circuits out of logic gates and see what they do.

How to use

  • Drag a part from the toolbar onto the board
  • Drag from one pin to another to wire them up
  • Click an IN switch to flip it between 0 and 1
  • Drag a wire off an input pin to disconnect it
  • Right-click something (or select it and press Delete) to remove it
  • Puzzles: build what the level asks for with the gates you're given, then press test

Welcome to Gate Playground

Logic gates are the tiny switches every computer is built from. Wire switches into gates and gates into lamps, then watch the signals light up as they flow through.
