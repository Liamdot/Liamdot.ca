Soko

made October 2026

Push a box onto every goal, then get every player to a finish. Boxes can be pushed but not pulled, and only one at a time.

How to play

  • The levels are doors on a map. Walk onto one and press Enter, Space or E (or enter) to play it
  • Solving a level frees the grey boxes that block the way to the next door. Push them out of the way to get on
  • Solving a level takes you back to the map, and map (Esc) leaves a level without solving it
  • Move with the arrow keys or WASD, and hold a direction to keep moving
  • On a phone, swipe the board or use the buttons below it
  • When a level has several players, they all move together. A player that's blocked stays where it is
  • The finishes are lowered grey flags until every goal has a box on it, then they go up and turn green. The level is won when every player is standing on a green flag
  • Some levels have more boxes than goals, so not every box has to be used
  • A box can go anywhere a player can, finishes and doors included. A box on a finish or a door has to be moved off before it can be used
  • The edge of a level is solid, like a wall
  • undo (Z) takes back a move, and restart (R) starts the level again. On the map, restart puts every box back where it started, in case one ends up in the way
  • my levels lists the levels you've made or saved

Making levels

  • make opens the editor. Paint walls, floor, goals, boxes, players and finishes; right-click paints floor
  • A level needs at least one finish for each player. Boxes and goals are optional: with no goals, the finishes are open from the start
  • check works out whether the puzzle can be solved
  • test plays it, and save to my levels keeps it in this browser
  • share makes a link that anyone can use to play it
  • Levels use the standard Sokoban text format, so levels from elsewhere can be pasted in
  • Clearing your save resets your progress and the map, but keeps your saved levels
