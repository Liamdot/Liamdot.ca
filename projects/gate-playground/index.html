Gate Playground

made September 2026

Build circuits out of logic gates and see what they do.

How to use

  • Drag a part from the toolbar onto the board
  • Drag from one pin to another to wire them up — thin pins carry one bit, square pins carry a whole byte
  • Click an IN switch to flip it; click the little cells on a BYTE to set a number
  • Right-click something (or select it and press Delete) to remove it

From an expression

Type something like (A'B + C) in the box and press build, and it gets wired up for you, switches labelled and all.

  • A', !A or NOT A for not
  • AB, A*B or A AND B for and
  • A+B or A OR B for or, A^B for exclusive or
  • NAND, NOR and XNOR work spelled out
  • On a puzzle it uses the switches and lamp already there, so you can build an answer and test it

Truth table

Press table to try every combination of the switches and write down what the lamps do, along with the minterms and maxterms.

Time

Memory parts only change when time moves. pause, step (one tick) and the speed slider control it; reset time forgets everything remembered.

Chips

Press save as chip to turn the board into a part you can place inside other circuits. Its IN and BYTE parts become its input pins, its OUT and SHOW parts its outputs, so name them first. Chips appear under my chips: double-click to edit, right-click to delete.

Welcome to Gate Playground

Logic gates are the tiny switches every computer is built from. Wire switches into gates and gates into lamps, then watch the signals light up as they flow through.
